## Interview Best Practices

### Before the Interview
1. **Research** the journalist's recent work
2. **Prepare** 3 key points you must convey
3. **Practice** technical explanations in simple terms
4. **Test** all technical equipment
5. **Review** latest student success stories

### During the Interview

#### DO:
- Lead with concrete examples and metrics
- Use analogies for complex concepts
- Share specific student outcomes
- Offer to demonstrate live
- Bridge back to key messages
- Be enthusiastic about the topic
- Admit what you don't know

#### DON'T:
- Use jargon without explanation
- Make unsubstantiated claims
- Criticize competitors directly
- Promise what you can't deliver
- Rush through explanations
- Assume technical knowledge

### Bridging Techniques
- "That's a great question, and what's really important here is..."
- "Yes, and the bigger picture shows..."
- "I understand the concern, and here's what we're seeing..."
- "Let me give you a concrete example..."
