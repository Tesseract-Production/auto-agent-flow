## Quick Tips for Using These Templates:

1. **Personalization is key** - Always reference specific recent work
2. **Lead with data** - Journalists prefer concrete metrics over vague claims
3. **Offer exclusive access** - First looks, private demos, embargoed data
4. **Make it timely** - Connect to current trends or recent news
5. **Keep initial email to 150-200 words** - Expand in follow-ups
6. **Best send times** - Tuesday-Thursday, 10 AM - 2 PM PT for Bay Area journalists
