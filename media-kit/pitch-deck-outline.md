## Presentation Tips

### Timing (15-minute pitch)

- Slides 1-4: 5 minutes (problem/solution)
- Slides 5-8: 5 minutes (market/product)
- Slides 9-12: 4 minutes (competition/team/ask)
- Slide 13: 1 minute (vision)

### Key Phrases to Emphasize

- "Production gap"
- "Definitive standard"
- "60% faster implementation"
- "$50K annual savings"

### Demo Points

- Show live workflow building
- Display student dashboard
- Highlight enterprise adoption
