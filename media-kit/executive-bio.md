# Executive Biography

## Vadim - Founder, Tesseract Nexus
### Full Stack Developer & Augmented AI Engineer

Vadim is a product creator and AI builder with 9 years of experience cultivating products and experiences in both AI-native and legacy software development. As an alumnus of Antler NYC and Founder Institute, and with previous experience at Plugin Alliance/Native Instruments, he brings a unique blend of technical expertise and business acumen to AI orchestration education.

Specializing in personalized and privacy-first Agentic Stacks, Vadim has built systems that produce higher quality outputs while being more customizable and significantly cheaper to operate and scale. His approach focuses on helping domain experts enrich their specialized knowledge with cutting-edge Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence capabilities.

At Tesseract Nexus, Vadim transforms his deep implementation experience into accessible education, helping over 10,000 automation professionals build production-ready AI systems. His teaching philosophy centers on providing the detailed, production-focused guidance he wished existed when starting his own journey—making complex AI orchestration accessible to builders at all levels.

"AI is the ultimate tool for productizing yourself into an extendable and autonomous system," says Vadim. "If you can dream it, I can help you make it."
